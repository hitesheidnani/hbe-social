Royalty-free instrumental MP3s for Reels and Shorts. Put each track in the folder for its mood:

  music/upbeat/   Top 10 countdown Reels (steady beat, light electronic)
  music/calm/     psychology and mindset cards (lo-fi, soft piano)
  music/news/     market update and US close cards (clean, corporate)

One track per mood per day, in rotation, with fade in and out. If a mood folder is empty any track in music/ is used;
with no tracks at all a soft generated tone is used.
